Discovered devices

Security

Set a password to require sign-in before viewing cameras on the local network. Leave blank to disable authentication.

Window

Open PTZLite in a dedicated app window — no address bar, tabs, or browser toolbar, with its own taskbar icon. Requires Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.

Appearance

Choose a look for the camera viewer. Saved on this device; applies next time the viewer opens. Light mode is available with Classic only.

Performance

Keep camera streams warm in the background so they open instantly. Each warm stream holds one connection open to your NVR; turn this down if the NVR struggles or you rarely switch streams.
