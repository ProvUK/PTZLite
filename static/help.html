First launch

After installing PTZLite, it appears in your Start Menu and system tray. When launched it starts a local web server and opens your browser automatically at http://localhost:8765.

On first launch you will see an empty grid — no cameras are configured yet. Click the Settings (gear) icon in the top-right of the header to add your first camera.

Tip: PTZLite runs entirely on your local network. No data is sent to the internet and no account is required.

Adding your first camera

  1. Click the Settings icon (⚙) in the header.
  2. Click Add Camera at the bottom of the camera list.
  3. Fill in the camera details — see Camera fields explained below.
  4. Click Test Connection to verify the RTSP stream is reachable before saving.
  5. Click Save. The camera will appear in the viewer immediately.
Tip: Make sure your camera is on the same local network as your PC and that RTSP is enabled in the camera's own settings.

Reolink cameras

Reolink cameras use the native Reolink HTTP API for PTZ commands. Set Brand to Reolink.

RTSP stream URLs

PTZLite builds the RTSP URL automatically using the format:

rtsp://username:password@ip:554/h264Preview_01_main

The channel number is added to the stream name (channel 0 = _01_, channel 1 = _02_, etc.).

Requirements

  • RTSP must be enabled in the camera's Network settings.
  • The camera must be on the same subnet as your PC.
  • Default RTSP port is 554 — do not change unless your camera uses a custom port.

Reolink Home Hub Pro

The Hub Pro is an NVR that connects cameras wirelessly. Each physical camera appears as a separate channel. Add one entry in PTZLite per camera channel.

Setup

  • Set IP to the Hub Pro's IP address (not the individual camera's IP — cameras are wireless and have no direct IP).
  • Set Channel to the camera's channel number as shown in the Reolink app (0-based: first camera = 0, second = 1, etc.).
  • Use the same admin username and password as the Hub Pro itself.

Recommended stream setting

Use Sub stream for Hub Pro cameras. The main stream uses H.265 which some browsers cannot decode. The sub stream uses H.264 and plays reliably in all browsers.

Tip: If you see a black screen or buffering on a Hub Pro camera, switch to Sub stream using the Main/Sub buttons in the header.

Dahua cameras

Dahua cameras use a different RTSP URL format and the CGI API for PTZ. Set Brand to Dahua.

PTZLite uses absolute position PTZ control for Dahua cameras, mirroring the behaviour of SmartPSS. Click-to-centre calculates the target pan/tilt from the current position and moves directly to it.

RTSP URL format used

rtsp://username:password@ip:554/cam/realmonitor?channel=1&subtype=0

subtype=0 is main stream, subtype=1 is sub stream.

Other ONVIF cameras

Any ONVIF-compatible camera can be added for live streaming. Set Brand to ONVIF.

PTZ support for ONVIF cameras uses the standard ONVIF ContinuousMove and RelativeMove commands. Results vary by camera — not all cameras implement the full ONVIF PTZ profile.

Note: Click-to-centre accuracy depends on the camera reporting its current position via ONVIF. Cameras that do not expose position feedback will use timed movement estimates which are less precise.

Grid layouts

Use the layout buttons in the header to choose how many cameras are shown at once:

1×11Single camera focus or fullscreen monitoring
2×12Side-by-side comparison of two cameras
2×24Standard 4-camera overview
3×26Medium installations
3×39Larger installations
4×416Large sites with many cameras

If you have more cameras than fit in the current layout, pagination arrows appear automatically — use them to move between pages.

When you first open the viewer, PTZLite automatically selects the best layout for your number of cameras.

Main vs Sub stream

The Main and Sub buttons in the header switch all cameras between their high-resolution and low-resolution streams.

  • Main stream — full resolution (e.g. 4K or 5MP). Higher bandwidth, may stutter on slower networks or with many cameras.
  • Sub stream — reduced resolution (typically 720p). Lower bandwidth, smoother with many cameras.

Switching streams reconnects all video feeds — expect a brief pause while streams restart.

Tip: If you are viewing 9 or 16 cameras simultaneously, Sub stream will give significantly smoother playback.

Fullscreen

Hover over any camera tile to reveal a fullscreen button in the top-right corner of that tile. Click it to expand that camera to fill the screen.

Press Esc or click the button again to exit fullscreen.

Pagination

When you have more cameras than the current layout can show, navigation arrows (‹ and ›) appear in the header alongside a page counter.

Click the arrows to move between pages. Each page shows a full grid of cameras.

Click to centre

For PTZ-enabled cameras, click anywhere on the camera image to pan and tilt so that point becomes the new centre of the frame.

A ripple animation confirms where you clicked. The camera moves and the view updates within a second or two depending on the camera's speed.

Tip: Click-to-centre works best on Reolink and Dahua cameras. ONVIF accuracy depends on the camera model.

Joystick drag

Click and hold on a PTZ camera image, then drag in any direction to move the camera continuously. Release to stop.

  • Drag distance from the start point controls speed — drag further for faster movement.
  • A ring and knob indicator appears to show the joystick position.
  • Release the mouse to stop the camera.

If you click and release quickly (without dragging), it is treated as a click-to-centre rather than a joystick command.

Presets

Camera presets are saved PTZ positions. If your camera has presets configured, they appear as buttons along the bottom of the camera tile.

Click a preset button to move the camera to that saved position instantly.

Presets are defined on the camera itself (via the Reolink app, SmartPSS, or the camera's web interface) and read automatically by PTZLite — you do not define them in PTZLite.

Light / Dark mode

Click the sun/moon icon in the header to switch between dark and light mode. Your preference is saved and remembered across sessions.

Stream health

The small dot in the header shows the status of the go2rtc stream engine:

  • Green — stream engine running normally.
  • Red — stream engine offline or unresponsive.
  • Grey — checking status.

Click the dot to force a reconnect. PTZLite also checks health every 15 seconds and restarts go2rtc automatically if it goes offline.

Individual cameras show an ⚠ Stream offline overlay if their specific stream stops. Click Reconnect on the overlay to restart all streams.

Troubleshooting

Black screen / buffering on camera

  • Switch to Sub stream — main stream may be H.265 which some browsers cannot decode (particularly on Reolink Hub Pro).
  • Check the camera is online and reachable by pinging its IP from your PC.
  • Check that RTSP is enabled in the camera's own settings.
  • Click the green/red dot in the header to restart the stream engine.

PTZ click does nothing

  • Confirm PTZ is enabled for that camera in Settings.
  • Check the camera's username and password are correct — PTZ uses the same credentials as streaming.
  • Ensure the camera is a PTZ model — fixed cameras cannot pan or tilt.

Camera added but not showing in viewer

  • Check the camera is set to Enabled in Settings.
  • If you recently added the camera, the viewer may need a browser refresh (F5).

App doesn't open on launch

  • Check the system tray — PTZLite may be running but the browser didn't open automatically. Right-click the tray icon and choose Open.
  • If the tray icon is missing, the app may have crashed on startup. Check for a startup_error.log file in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\PTZLite\.
  • Port 8765 may be in use by another application. Check the log file for a port conflict error.

Stream engine shows red / streams not loading

  • Click the red dot to attempt a restart.
  • If it stays red, check ptzlite.log in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\PTZLite\ for error details.
  • Antivirus software may be blocking go2rtc — add an exception for the PTZLite install folder.
